The U.S. caregiving sector includes both unpaid family care and a large professional workforce,
with over 3.4 million home care workers,
and tens of millions of unpaid caregivers providing essential support.


Key Takeaways for Caregiver Companies
Caregiver companies must address:

High demand for both professional and family care.

Workforce diversity and varied education levels.

Need for flexible, home‑based services to support independent living.

Well‑being and retention strategies to combat caregiver stress and turnover.

Integration of technology for monitoring, medication management, and care coordination.
